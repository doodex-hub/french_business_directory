What lands on the contact

Nine Odoo contact fields, filled from the official French register

Twelve if you also run a department module. Here is every field the module writes, grouped by the job it does — and flagged where it matters.

Legal identity

3 fields · always

The three values a French invoice is legally built on. Pull them from the register once and accounting stops chasing the customer for their paperwork.

  • ✓
    Name nameThe official company name. Stays your commercial display name.
  • ✓
    Social Reason social_reasonThe registered legal name your invoices must carry. Added by this module and tracked in the chatter, so every change is logged.
  • ✓
    SIRET siretThe 14-digit establishment number, written into Odoo’s own French field from l10n_fr.
A SIRET is the company’s 9-digit SIREN plus a 5-digit establishment code. Because the machine writes it, it is the register’s SIRET — there is no keyboard in between and nothing to transpose.

Address & map position

6 fields · always

The registered address exactly as the register spells it, plus the coordinates that put the contact on a map without a geocoding subscription.

  • ✓
    Street streetNumber and street name.
  • ✗
    Street 2 street2Cleared, not filled. The module empties this field when it writes the address — the one thing to check before you confirm.
  • ✓
    ZIP zipPostal code.
  • ✓
    City cityCommune.
  • ✓
    Geo Latitude partner_latitudeStraight from the register, so no geocoding service is called.
  • ✓
    Geo Longitude partner_longitudeWritten as 0 when the register holds no coordinates — check before you route a van by it.

Region

3 fields · conditional

Filled only if your database already has a module providing res.country.department. Without it these three are simply never touched, and the other nine still fill normally.

  • •
    Department country_department_idMatched on the first two digits of the postal code.
  • •
    State state_idTaken from that department lookup.
  • •
    Country country_idSame lookup. The module never sets Country on its own.
Two digits is a blunt match: Corsica (2A / 2B) and the overseas departments do not resolve, and when the lookup finds nothing these three are written empty, which clears whatever was there.
✓Filled from the register✗Cleared by the module•Needs a department module

Selecting a result overwrites these fields rather than merging them — which is why Odoo asks you to confirm first. Everything outside this list, your own custom fields included, is left untouched.

02/07Features

Thirteen things it does, in plain words

Everything below is in the module you download. Nothing here is a roadmap item.

Search from inside the contact form

A Business Directory button next to the company name, on the standard Odoo contact form. Nothing to open, nothing to configure.

The search runs on open

The wizard uses the name already on the contact, so the first 25 results are on screen before you type anything.

Official French register as the source

Company data comes from the French government's public company search service — the same register the official directory site uses.

25 results per page, with a real total

The header shows how many companies matched in total, not just how many are on screen.

Prev / Next pagination that never dead-ends

Move through pages inside the same window. Past the last page, Next loops to page one; before page one, Prev jumps to the last.

Up to 100 establishments per company

Groups with many branches show the establishments matching your search, so you can pick the site you actually deal with. Paging forward keeps the full 100; paging backwards currently returns fewer.

Trading-status badge

Green for an establishment still active, red with the closing date for one that has shut. You see it before you send a quotation.

Creation date on every establishment

Each establishment row carries the date the register recorded it, so a brand-new entity is easy to spot.

Registered legal name, kept separate

A new Social Reason field holds the official legal name, so your commercial display name stays yours.

Change history in the chatter

Social Reason is a tracked field: every change is logged on the contact like any other tracked Odoo field.

Confirmation before overwrite

Odoo asks “are you sure” before it replaces existing values. Cancel and the contact is untouched.

Geo coordinates included

Latitude and longitude arrive with the address, written to Odoo's standard geo fields — no geocoding service is called. The Odoo map view that displays them is an Enterprise feature.

Company contacts only

The button is hidden on individual contacts, so the wizard cannot be opened where it makes no sense.

French translation included

A French translation file (i18n/fr.po) ships with the module.

06/07FAQ

Thirteen questions buyers actually ask

If yours is not here, e-mail it to odoo@doodex.net and we will add it.

Where does the company data come from?+

From recherche-entreprises.api.gouv.fr, the French government's public company search service. It is the same official register that the public company directory website is built on, so the SIRET, legal name, address and trading status you see are the register's own values — not a Doodex copy of them.

Do I need an API key, an account or a subscription? +

No. The service is public and the module calls it directly from your Odoo server. There is nothing to sign up for, nothing to renew and no per-lookup charge.

Is the module really free? What is the catch?+

It is free and published under LGPL-3, with the source in the download. Doodex builds and maintains it because a working French contact record is the starting point of most Odoo projects we are hired for — and because a module that people actually use is the best introduction to our work. There is no paid tier of this module hiding behind a wall.

Does my data leave my database?+

Your Odoo server sends the company name you are searching to the government service and reads back the public register entry. That is the only outbound traffic. Nothing is sent to Doodex, no telemetry is collected, and every result is written into your own database.

Which Odoo versions does it support?+

This listing is Odoo 18.0, Community and Enterprise, module version 18.0.1.0.0. If you are on a different major version, e-mail us before you install — a migration is straightforward work, but this build targets 18.0 and we will not pretend otherwise.

Why does it depend on l10n_fr?+

Because l10n_fr (the France — Accounting app) is what adds the SIRET field to Odoo contacts. Without it there would be nowhere to write the SIRET. It ships with Odoo, so there is nothing extra to buy.

Exactly which fields get overwritten?+

Name, Social Reason, SIRET, Street, Street 2, ZIP, City, Geo Latitude and Geo Longitude — plus Department, State and Country if a module providing res.country.department is installed. The full list is in the table further up this page. Odoo shows a confirmation dialog before writing, and every other field on the contact is untouched.

What is “Social Reason” and why is it separate from the name? +

Social Reason is the registered legal name of the company — the one that must appear on a French invoice. It is often not what people call the business day to day. The module adds it as its own field so your commercial display name in Name stays yours, and it is a tracked field, so changes are logged in the contact's chatter.

A company has eleven branches. Which one does it fill? +

Whichever you pick, out of the establishments that match your search — up to 100 per company. Each carries its own SIRET, address, creation date and status badge, so you can attach the branch you invoice instead of accepting the head office by default. If the register returns no matching establishment, you get the head office.

Does it give me the SIREN as well as the SIRET? +

Indirectly, and reliably: a SIRET is the 9-digit SIREN of the company followed by the 5-digit code of the establishment. The module writes the full 14-digit SIRET of the establishment you selected, so the SIREN is its first nine digits. There is no separate SIREN field on the Odoo contact for it to fill.

Can I update all my existing contacts in one go? +

Not in this version. It is a per-contact tool: open a record, search, confirm. If you need a batch or scheduled enrichment over thousands of contacts, that is custom work — tell us how many records and we will quote it.

What happens if the government service is unreachable? +

Straight answer: today you get an Odoo error dialog rather than a friendly message. Close it and retry once connectivity is back — nothing is written to your contact. A proper “the register is unavailable” message is the first item in the next release. We would rather tell you that here than have you discover it after installing.

Will it work on Odoo Online (SaaS)?+

No. Odoo Online does not accept third-party modules of this kind, so this module is for Odoo.sh, on-premise or any self-managed hosting. If you are on Odoo Online, e-mail us and we will tell you honestly what your options are.
